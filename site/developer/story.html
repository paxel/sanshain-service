One team, start to finish. The payments Producer publishes its first spec, the orders Consumer pins it, a forgotten version bump and a breaking change are refused, a snapshot is iterated and promoted, a release is cut, the catalog is pushed, and an AI assistant asks about the result. Every exchange below was recorded against a fresh 2.6.1 instance by scripts/record-walkthroughs.sh; the graph on the right of each step is the catalog as it stands at that point.

How to read this page

Use Previous and Next, the step chips, or the arrow keys; #step-N in the address opens a step directly. Each step says what the team does, shows the recorded answer (an HTTP exchange, a client transcript or a screenshot), and links to the chapter and the reference entry it uses. Tokens are redacted and the instance's address is shown as https://sanshain.corp; everything else is as the service answered it.

Behind the story



  The single tasks of this story, and a few the story does not tell, are the
  Developer recipes. The operator who installed this instance has
  a story of their own. To re-record both against a newer
  release, run just record-walkthroughs in the repository: it builds the service,
  starts a fresh instance, replays every step with the real clients (cargo-sanshain, the JavaScript
  client, the Go client, the Conan client, the Maven plugin) and curl, compares every
  answer with what the step expects, and overwrites site/evidence/.
