Three chapters for a new reader: one to run it, one to understand it, one to look things up.

  • Chapter 1

    Getting started

    Docker with SQLite or PostgreSQL, or from source. First login, approving users, a token, the first provide and require by hand, then with a client.

  • Chapter 2

    Concepts

    Producers and Consumers, version lines, snapshot and GA, pins and resolution, the graph with trunk and release branches, periphery, token scopes, webhooks.

  • Chapter 3

    Glossary

    Every term in one or two sentences. Chapters link here on the first use of a term.

After that, the Developer track is for integrating a build, the Operator track for running the service, and the Reference for the contracts.

Checked against v2.6.0.
